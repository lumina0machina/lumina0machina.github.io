About me

  • I'm a small 'bedroom' musician that started out making music in 2025. My "journey" to get here so to speak was a little odd, with me mainly practicing drum programming until late 2025 since I was still largely unfamiliar with essentially everything surrounding this topic, but also due to not having access to many songwriting tools, more specifically VST's (Virtual Studio Technology). Thankfully as of late 2026, I've gotten a lot more familiar with everything surrounding making music and have more access to more tools that finally allowed me to start making 'proper' music if you will. I think the first release/project I would say that's 'proper' to me where I did everything alone was machina, which is also still one of my favorite things I've made so far!

    My music taste largely revolves around Extreme Metal and its various sub/microgenres, though I also enjoy things that aren't strictly metal; an example would be hardcore punk related things like say, grindcore and crust punk (and their various subgenres) but also things like artcore/speecore (my favorite producer of which is Sakuzyo, they do phenomenal work, especially for the various Cry-Series of games which are a large influence on me and my music).

    I'm always interested in learning more about quite literally everything regarding music, even if it's not something I would usually listen to. An example here I suppose could be the phenomenal Allan Holdsworth (if you're unfamiliar, I highly encourage you to go read about him, he's one of the most interesting people ever), even though I do actually really enjoy his music so that's maybe not the best example, but oh well.

    You can find more on that if you're interested through my Rateyourmusic profile that's linked in my socials.
