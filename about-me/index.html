a little about me

I usually go by lumina, and use it/its pronouns. I'm a small 'bedroom' musician that started out making music in 2025. I mostly practied drum programming until early 2026, which is when I actually started to write 'proper' music if you will.

interests

music

My music taste largely revolves around Extreme Metal and its various sub/microgenres, though I also enjoy things that aren't strictly metal; an example would be hardcore punk related things like say, grindcore and crust punk (and their various subgenres) but also things like artcore/speecore (my favorite producer of which is Sakuzyo, they do phenomenal work, especially for the various Cry-Series of games which are a large influence on me and my music).

I'm always interested in learning more about essentially everything regarding music. An example here I suppose could be the phenomenal Allan Holdsworth (if you're unfamiliar, I highly encourage you to go read about him, he's one of the most interesting people ever). I also really enjoy learning more about various pieces of gear, and weird instruments, by extension also the music made with those.

You can find more on that if you're interested through my RateYourMusic profile.

other

Aside from music, I also deeply love technology and everything surrounding it. It's something I've been interested in essentially all my life, though my love for it has kind of gone down over the past few years. This is mainly due to AI and how it's getting shoved down everyone's throat even though the majority of people either don't care about or want anything to do with it. This is also probably a good point to mention that I am *fully* against AI creeping into every conceivable form of art, and if you're someone that's contributing to that, please just stop doing that. Music is a form of art, and art is an extension of oneself, which AI does inherently not possess, or understand.

I am also fascinated by the Hardware that goes into every piece of Tech that we have, one of my favorite hobbies used to be taking apart virtually every electronic device I owned, which I still enjoy doing from time to time. I also want to learn soldering one day since that plays into this interest of mine.
